Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This page explains what data this website processes, why, and what control you have.

What we collect

The calculator does not send your numbers anywhere. Every figure you type — prices, costs, VAT rates — is calculated inside your own browser. Nothing is transmitted to us, nothing is stored, and nothing is linked to you. Closing the page erases it.

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for an email address or any personal detail to use this site.

Cookies, storage and consent

On your first visit you are asked to choose. Nothing outside the essential category is stored until you agree, and refusing is exactly as easy as accepting — the site works identically either way.

Before you make a choice, consent for advertising and analytics storage defaults to denied, so no such cookies are set. If you accept, that consent is passed to advertising partners in the standard Consent Mode format.

  • To change or withdraw your choice at any time, use the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page.
  • You can also opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies via aboutads.info.
  • Blocking or deleting cookies in your browser does not stop the calculator from working — it runs entirely in your browser and needs no cookies at all.

Analytics

If analytics are used, they are aggregate and privacy-respecting: how many people visit a page, and roughly which country they are in. They are not used to identify individuals, and they are not enabled until you consent.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to processing, or to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because we do not collect personal data through the calculator, most such requests relate to cookies set by advertising partners, which can be controlled through the opt-out links above.

Children

This site is aimed at adults running or considering an online shop, and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about how your data is handled, can be sent to contact@craftfee.com.
