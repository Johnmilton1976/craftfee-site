Why Etsy charges fees on your shipping

Etsy's fee bases decide this, and only one line sits outside them:

FeeCharged on
Transaction fee (6.5%)Item + shipping + gift wrap (not sales tax)
Payment processing (3–4% + fixed)Item + shipping + gift wrap + sales tax
Regulatory operating fee (some countries)Item + shipping + gift wrap
Offsite Ads (15% or 12%)The whole order — item, shipping and gift wrap
Shipping is inside every base except sales tax. Whatever you charge for postage — or swallow to offer "free" shipping — carries the same percentages as the item. A $7.95 shipping charge is not $7.95 of pure cost; it is $7.95 that the transaction fee, processing and the regulatory fee all take a slice of before the label is paid.

The break-even shipping charge is lower than most sellers think

Divide the postage cost by one minus Etsy's percentage cut. A US seller buying a $5.60 label and $0.60 of packaging breaks even at $6.85 — 9.5% of the charge goes to Etsy, so $6.20 is what has to be left over. Charge the label cost plus a flat dollar instead ($6.60) and every order quietly loses 23 cents.

Two things move that number:

  • Offsite Ads. A 15% ad fee lands on the shipping too, which takes the break-even to $8.21 in the US. Sellers who price shipping off organic orders lose money on every ad-attributed sale without noticing, because the loss sits inside the postage line rather than the item line.
  • VAT on Etsy's fees. Outside the US, Etsy charges VAT or GST on its own fee. A UK seller's effective cut rises from 10.98% to 13.18%, and the break-even charge goes from $6.96 to $7.14.
Rule of thumb: shipping charge = (label + packaging) ÷ 0.87 for the UK and most of the eurozone, ÷ 0.90 in the US and Canada, ÷ 0.755 if the order arrived through Offsite Ads. Round up to the next 50 cents and you have a charge that never loses money.

Free shipping versus charged shipping: identical fees

A $38 item with $7.95 shipping and a $45.95 item with "free" shipping cost the same in Etsy fees, because both are charged on the same $45.95 order total. Free shipping changes who pays the postage, not whether Etsy takes a percentage of it.

Price the postage in, offer free shipping above your break-even order value, and stop treating the shipping line as money Etsy cannot see. Dimensional weight: the second reason a shipping charge misses

A parcel is billed on the greater of its scale weight and its dimensional weight — cubic size in inches divided by the carrier's divisor, 139 for UPS and FedEx and 166 for most USPS retail services. A 12 × 10 × 8 inch box is 960 cubic inches, which is 6.9 lb at 139. Fill it with a 5 oz cushion and the carrier still prices 7 lb.

This is where handmade sellers get caught, because the products that need protecting are light and bulky: ceramics, framed prints, dried flowers, anything with air in the box. Measure the packed box, not the product, and check the billable weight before you set the shipping profile. The box in the calculator above is that exact 12 × 10 × 8 case — change the numbers to your own carton.

What to do with the number

  • Set the shipping profile from the break-even charge, not from the label. The label is what you pay the carrier; the charge has to cover Etsy's percentage of it too.
  • Check the ad-attributed case separately. If Offsite Ads is on, the break-even is roughly 10% higher. A profile priced for organic orders bleeds on ad orders.
  • Fold shipping into the price above your break-even order value. Free shipping above $35, charged below it, is the usual compromise — it wins the ranking signal where the order can absorb the cost.
  • Keep packaging in the number. Sixty cents a parcel is $600 on a thousand orders, and it is the line sellers forget when they compare the charge against the label.

Quick answers

Does Etsy charge the transaction fee on shipping?

Yes — 6.5% on item plus shipping plus gift wrap. Only sales tax sits outside that base.

If I offer free shipping, do I still pay fees on it?

You pay fees on the postage you swallow, because Etsy charges on the order total regardless of which line the money came from. Free shipping moves the cost; it does not remove it.

Is the Offsite Ads fee charged on shipping?

Yes, on the whole order including shipping and gift wrap, capped at US$100 per order. The Offsite Ads page has the threshold and the opt-out window.

Does buying the label through Etsy change the fees?

No. Etsy sells shipping labels at the carrier rate without adding a markup, but buying the label from Etsy does not reduce the fees charged on the shipping you collected. Where you buy the label changes what you pay the carrier, not what Etsy takes.
