Etsy's 2026 fee schedule at a glance

FeeRateCharged on
Listing fee$0.20Each listing or renewal, plus each extra unit sold
Transaction fee6.5%Item + shipping + gift wrap (excludes sales tax)
Payment processing3% + $0.25 (US)Item + shipping + gift wrap + sales tax
Regulatory operating fee0 – 1.97%Item + shipping + gift wrap, in some countries only
Offsite Ads15% or 12%The whole order, capped at US$100 per order

The three mandatory fees stack to roughly 11% of what the buyer pays on a typical US order, before advertising. Add an Offsite Ads order and the same sale passes 25%. That gap between 11% and 25% is the single most useful thing to understand about selling on Etsy.

Etsy sets processing by the country of your bank account, and in Canada and Australia the rate is one point higher on orders from abroad. Regulatory operating fees changed on 22 June 2026 — France is now 1.14% and Hungary 1.97%, while the US, Germany and Australia still carry none. Verify your own rate in Etsy's Fees & Payments Policy before pricing anything.
