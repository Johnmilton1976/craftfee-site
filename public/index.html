What Etsy actually takes

Etsy charges four or five separate fees on a single sale, and they are not all calculated on the same amount. The Etsy fee calculator works out the real numbers in a few seconds, and these guides explain each fee line by line — so you can price with confidence instead of guessing.

Etsy's 2026 fee schedule at a glance

FeeRateCharged on
Listing fee$0.20Each listing or renewal, plus each extra unit sold
Transaction fee6.5%Item + shipping + gift wrap (excludes sales tax)
Payment processing3% + $0.25 (US)Item + shipping + gift wrap + sales tax
Regulatory operating fee0 – 1.97%Item + shipping + gift wrap, in some countries only
VAT or GST on Etsy's fees0 – 23%Every fee above, outside the US — stop it with a VAT ID
Offsite Ads15% or 12%The whole order, capped at US$100 per order
Sales tax on goods (US)0%Buyer pays it; Etsy collects and remits it in most states
Currency conversion2.5%Your payout, when listing currency ≠ bank currency

The three mandatory fees stack to roughly 11% of what the buyer pays on a typical US order, before advertising. Add an Offsite Ads order and the same sale passes 25%. That gap between 11% and 25% is the single most useful thing to understand about selling on Etsy.

Etsy sets processing by the country of your bank account, and in Canada and Australia the rate is one point higher on orders from abroad. Regulatory operating fees changed on 22 June 2026 — France is now 1.14% and Hungary 1.97%, while the US, Germany and Australia still carry none. Verify your own rate in Etsy's Fees & Payments Policy before pricing anything. All amounts below are in each seller's own local currency, so compare the percentages, not the absolute figures.

Three effects separate the top row from the bottom: European processing is one point higher, four of these countries add a regulatory fee, and outside the US Etsy taxes its own fees. The spread — 9.9% against 14.5% — is not a rounding difference. It is nearly five points of margin that a seller in France has to price back and a seller in the US never sees.

Assuming no VAT ID on file, so the tax on Etsy's fees is included. Canada uses the 5% federal GST floor — HST provinces pay more. The VAT on fees page explains which countries a VAT ID switches this line off in, and the calculator models your own numbers in any of the ten.
